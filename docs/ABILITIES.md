# What everything does: abilities, in plain words

## The whole game in four rules
1. **Level** decides *when* you can make something (every recipe has a level; see the table at the end).
2. **Station** decides *where* you make it: the Workbench and Kitchen from the start, then the Smelter (Lv 8), Forge (Lv 10), Dwarven Forge (Lv 20) and Star Anvil (Lv 35).
3. **Tier** decides *what it can beat*: Wood (1) → Iron (2) → Steel (3) → Mythic (4). Gear at or above a target's tier works at full strength. Below it, it barely scratches.
4. **Weapons hit monsters, tools hit obstacles, armor takes blows, amulets change your matches.**

---

## Amulets (you wear one at a time)

| Amulet | Gem | Power | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rootstone** | Emerald | **Earth:** your matches damage *any* obstacle beside them, even ones that normally need a tool (rock, iron veins, coral, stumps). | Boards full of obstacles; forgetting to bring the right tool |
| **Tidestone** | Sapphire | **Tide:** every match of 4+ and every chain reaction gives **+1 extra ⚡**; blasts give +1 more. | Swinging your weapon more often; bosses |
| **Emberstone** | Ruby | **Fury:** when a match hits a monster beside it, that hit does **+2 damage**. | Monster-heavy lands and raids |
| **Heart of Stone** | (boss reward: Golem) | **Stone:** **+8 max ❤️**. | Long fights, staying alive |
| **Siren's Pearl** | (boss reward: Siren) | **Siren:** every match of 4+ **heals 1 ❤️**. | Healing without food |

**In one line each:** emerald = break things, sapphire = more energy, ruby = hit harder, Heart of Stone = more health, Siren's Pearl = heal as you play.

## Weapons (hit monsters)

**Damage per swing** depends on the tier: Wood 3, Iron 6, Steel 10, Mythic 15. You also get +1 for every 5 levels.
- **Too weak?** If the monster's armor is above your weapon's tier, you only do ½, then ¼, then ⅛ of the damage.
- **Energy cost:** a Wood weapon costs 1 ⚡ to swing; Iron and up cost 2 ⚡.

| Type | Special ability |
|---|---|
| **Battle-axe** | **Cleave:** the monsters next to your target also take half damage. |
| **Sword** | **+1 damage** on every hit. |
| **Bow** | Hits **every monster in the column**. |
| **Coral spear** (sea) | Hits the monsters **above and below** too. Counts as one tier stronger against sea creatures (crabs, the Siren). No forge needed. |
| **Pearl sabre** (sea) | **Light:** swings cost **1 less ⚡**, plus +1 damage. |
| **Grak's war-axe** (boss) | An Iron battle-axe you win instead of forging. |
| **Goblin shiv** (raid spoil) | An Iron sword you win from goblins. |

## Tools (hit obstacles and camp defenses)

| Tool | Breaks |
|---|---|
| **Wood-axe** | Stumps, thornbushes, kelp, wooden defenses |
| **Pick** | Boulders, ore veins, coral, stone defenses |
| **Hammer** | Any built defense on raids: walls, gates, towers, keeps |
| **Dwarven pick** (raid spoil) | A Steel pick you win from dwarves |

## Armor (takes the blows)

When something hits you, the blow lands on your **body 40%**, **head 20%**, **legs 20%** or **shield 20%** of the time (the body takes the shield's share if you have none).
- **Armor tier above the attacker's weapon:** the blow is **blocked completely**.
- **Same tier:** you take **40%** of the damage.
- **One tier below:** you take **75%**.
- **No armor there, or two tiers below:** you take full damage.

Each blow wears that piece down. It lasts 4 / 8 / 14 / 25 blows by tier; repair it in the Workshop for half its materials.

| Piece | Ability |
|---|---|
| Helmet, Body, Legs | Protect where they're worn |
| Shield | Catches its share of blows. The **Elderwood ward-shield** also stops dragonfire. |
| **Gloves** | **Never get hit.** They only add **⚡ at the start of every board** (+1 per tier). |
| **Shellback buckler** (sea) | Iron-strength shield made from crab shells. No forge needed. |

## Potions (Kitchen cauldron)

Brewed in the Kitchen, banked, then packed at camp like food (each takes a backpack slot). Tap one in the board tray to drink it.

| Potion | Effect | Strengths unlock at |
|---|---|---|
| **Sleeping draught** | Monsters can't strike you for 2 / 3 / 4 moves | Lv 6 / 16 / 26 |
| **Quicksilver tonic** | +5 ⚡ / +10 ⚡ / fills ⚡ to full | Lv 9 / 19 / 29 |
| **Giant's brew** | Your next 3 weapon swings hit ×1.5 / ×2 / ×2.5 | Lv 12 / 22 / 32 |

## The Harvest Fair
- **Golden tickets** blow onto about one gathering trip in three. Match next to one to grab it.
- A ticket plays one **fairground round**: 15 moves, no monsters. Every 3 gold ★ tokens matched wins a **wheel spin** (at least 1 per round).
- The **Prize Wheel** gives flair (hats and capes, just for looks), meals, gold, another golden ticket, or rarely the **Golden Horseshoe** (rare finds +15%). Plus one free spin each day you visit.

## Food

- **Everything you gather goes to the bank** (the Storehouse at 🏰 Your Camp). Before a trip, tap food there to **pack it**. Packed food takes up **backpack slots**. On a board, one tap of 🍖 eats the packed food that best fits your missing health. Tap packed food at camp to unpack it.
- Raids and bosses no longer take food on the way in.
- **Raw food** heals: Berries/Apples/Eggs 2 · Herring/Mushrooms/Meat 3 · Salmon/Honey 5 · Swordfish 8 · Golden kingfish 12.
- **Meals** are cooked in the Kitchen, go to the bank, and are packed the same way:

| Meal | Heals | Level |
|---|---|---|
| Trail bread | 5 | 1 |
| Fish chowder | 8 | 3 |
| Roast chicken | 10 | 5 |
| Honey cake | 12 | 9 |
| Salmon & mushroom pie | 15 | 12 |
| Farmhouse pie | 18 | 15 |
| Swordfish steak | 20 | 20 |
| Kingfish feast | 40 | 28 |

## Raids and camp

| Item | Ability |
|---|---|
| **War drum** / **Dwarf ale** | Use on a raid: their defenders wait **3 moves longer** before striking. *(Same effect; see the clean-up list.)* |
| **Old King's Crown** | On raids defenders wait 2 moves longer; opens the way to Emberwyrm. |
| **Runestone** | Placed in camp: +1 HP to every defense touching it. |
| Fence / Wall / Palisade / Watchtower / Gate / Bulwark / Pit trap | Camp defenses; tougher material needs better attacker gear. The palisade costs attackers a move per hit, the tower can push attackers back, and the trap costs them a move. |

## Why it feels complicated, and the simplest fixes

1. **Gloves are armor that never gets hit.** Players expect gloves to protect them. *Fix:* call them **Gauntlets of Vigour** (or similar) and describe them only as "+⚡ at the start". Or let the hands take some blows.
2. **The sword's "+1 damage" is dull** next to the axe and bow. *Fix:* give it something distinct, like a higher chance of a critical hit.
3. **Two items do the same thing** (drum and ale) and **two do "stops dragonfire"** (elderwood ward and ward-shield). *Fix:* merge them.
4. **Hammers overlap** with the axe and pick on raids. *Fix:* drop hammers; the axe breaks wooden defenses and the pick breaks stone and iron ones.
5. **Coal and charcoal** are two fuels. *Fix:* keep only coal, and have steel cost coal + oak.
6. **Food felt random.** *Fix (done):* nothing is eaten automatically any more; you pack food at camp (it uses backpack space) and one tap eats the best fit.
