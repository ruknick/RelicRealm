# Roadmap: ideas saved for later

Notes on upgrades to build and things to streamline. Nothing here is built yet.

## 1. A longer story that teaches the game

**The problem now:** there are 6 chapters, and the jump between them is huge.
- Chapter 3 ends around level 10–14.
- Chapter 4 then asks for all 5 relic shards, and each shard needs a land at **Lv 35**, which is about 50 trips per land.
- The story also starts talking about the dragon long before the player has steel, let alone mythic gear.
- The Golden Fields, the bosses (Grak, the Golem, the Siren), amulets and the Smelter/Forge are never part of the story.

**The fix:**
- Many more, shorter chapters, each about 2–4 levels long.
- Each chapter teaches **one new thing** and ends with a reward that leads into the next.
- The dragon stays a rumour until the Mythic era.
- **Shards come from story milestones** (each boss and each top-rank raid) instead of only from Lv 35 lands. That way the Crown builds up gradually across the whole game.

## 3. Gameplay ideas not built yet
- **Board shapes and harder trips:** non-rectangular boards, plus an occasional "hard" trip with better loot.
- **Collection book:** monsters, rare finds and map pieces, filled in as you discover them.
- **Daily bonus land:** one land a day gives double loot.
- **Sea monster level and the hidden island** off the coast.
- **New lands beyond the realm:** drowned swamp, Deep Tunnels, frozen peaks, haunted barrows (the Wayfarer's Chart already teases these).
- **Companions:** a helper that acts every few moves.
- **Replace the last emoji** (energy/health meters, bounty icons, some item icons) with hand-drawn art.
